Why These Calculations?

The ML model predicts panel efficiency (0–1), not raw kW. This is multiplied by your home's system capacity to give realistic output:

  • 1 BHK → 1 kWp system → max ~5 kWh/day
  • 2 BHK → 2 kWp system → max ~10 kWh/day
  • 3 BHK → 3 kWp system → max ~15 kWh/day

Savings = units solar covers × your entered tariff (₹/kWh). CO₂ offset = units × 0.82 kg (India grid factor).

Climate Impact

For every kWh generated by solar, 0.82 kg of CO₂ is avoided from the India electricity grid. SolarSmart helps households:

  • ✦ Offset electricity bills with rooftop solar generation
  • ✦ Identify ideal panel tilt angles for maximum efficiency
  • ✦ Quantify carbon savings in tree-equivalent terms
  • ✦ Schedule heavy appliances during peak solar windows
